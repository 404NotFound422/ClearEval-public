Extracted hours: 75.0

**Chosen Method:** iDISCO+  
**Chosen Labeling:** [c-Fos (Npas4) + Alexa Fluor 647, DAPI]  
**Justification:** iDISCO+ is well-suited for whole mouse brain clearing due to its high fluorescence preservation, compatibility with lipid-soluble dyes, and ability to maintain fine cellular structures. The use of c-Fos (Npas4) as a marker for activated neurons combined with DAPI for nuclear labeling allows for single-cell resolution imaging. This method supports long-term sample stability and is compatible with light-sheet microscopy, ideal for generating 3D distribution maps.

**Protocol Steps:**

1 Tissue Optical Clearing  
1.1 Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
1.2 Incubation with 40% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
1.3 Incubation with 60% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
1.4 Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
1.5 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  

2 Labeling  
2.1 Incubation with 1:500 anti-c-Fos (Npas4) antibody in 100% methanol (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 48 hours)  
2.2 Incubation with 1:500 Alexa Fluor 647-conjugated secondary antibody in 100% methanol (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 24 hours)  
2.3 Incubation with 1:1000 DAPI in 100% methanol (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  

3 Delipidation  
3.1 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) dibenzyl ether (DBE) (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
3.2 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) DBE (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
3.3 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) DBE (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  

4 Refractive Index Matching  
4.1 Incubation with RI-matching solution (1.48 RI, e.g., 10% (vol/vol) polyethylene glycol 350, 10% (vol/vol) ethylene glycol, 80% (vol/vol) PBS) (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
4.2 Incubation with RI-matching solution (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
4.3 Incubation with RI-matching solution (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  

5 Sample Storage and Imaging Preparation  
5.1 Transfer the cleared brain into a glass-bottom dish filled with RI-matching solution (Temperature: RT, Time: ongoing)  
5.2 Store at 4 ℃ in the dark until imaging  
5.3 For imaging, ensure the brain is fully immersed in RI-matching solution and mounted on a light-sheet or confocal microscope platform.
