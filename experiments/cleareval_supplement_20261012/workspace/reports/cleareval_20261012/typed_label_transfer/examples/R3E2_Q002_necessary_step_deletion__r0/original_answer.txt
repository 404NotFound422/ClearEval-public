**Chosen Method:** FDISCO
**Chosen Labeling:** [GFP (内源)]
**Justification:** FDISCO is highly optimal for intact adult mouse whole brains as it provides excellent transparency while significantly preserving endogenous fluorescent proteins (like GFP). For constructing a whole-brain 3D morphological map of neuronal somas, axons, and dendrites, utilizing endogenous GFP (e.g., via transgenic labeling or viral tracing) bypasses the severe penetration limitations and long incubation times of whole-brain immunolabeling. FDISCO's low-temperature THF dehydration effectively prevents fluorescence quenching, and its solvent-based RI matching achieves the deep transparency required for whole-brain light-sheet imaging.

**Protocol Steps:**

1. Incubation with 50% (vol/vol) tetrahydrofuran (THF) aqueous solution in a glass vial with gentle shaking. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
2. Incubation with 70% (vol/vol) THF aqueous solution with gentle shaking. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
3. Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) THF aqueous solution with gentle shaking. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
4. Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) pure THF with gentle shaking. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
5. Incubation with fresh 100% (vol/vol) pure THF with gentle shaking. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
