**Chosen Method:** FDISCO
**Chosen Labeling:** [GFP (内源), DAPI]
**Justification:** FDISCO is specifically selected because it is a solvent-based clearing method that provides excellent transparency for 3 mm brain blocks while uniquely utilizing low temperature (4 ℃) and alkaline pH adjustment (pH 9.0 via TEA) during dehydration. This specifically prevents the quenching of weak endogenous fluorescent proteins (like GFP), allowing for high-resolution 3D morphological analysis of fine dendritic structures and long-term storage (months) without signal loss. The dual-target strategy utilizes the preserved endogenous GFP to trace sparse neurons, while DAPI serves as a nuclear counterstain to provide essential 3D anatomical context without requiring complex immunolabeling that might damage the tissue.

**Protocol Steps:**

1. Incubation with 1x PBS to remove residual fixative.(Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours) Incubation with DAPI (10 µg/mL) in 1x PBS containing 0.2% (vol/vol) Triton X-100.(Temperature: RT, Time: 48 hours)
2. Incubation with 1x PBS to wash out excess DAPI dye.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours) Incubation with 50% (vol/vol) THF aqueous solution (pH adjusted to 9.0 with TEA).(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 6 hours)
3. Incubation with 70% (vol/vol) THF aqueous solution (pH adjusted to 9.0 with TEA).(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 6 hours) Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) THF aqueous solution (pH adjusted to 9.0 with TEA).(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 6 hours)
4. Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) THF (pH adjusted to 9.0 with TEA).(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 6 hours) Incubation with fresh 100% (vol/vol) THF (pH adjusted to 9.0 with TEA).(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
5. Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) DBE (Refractive Index = 1.56) without shaking.(Temperature: RT, Time: 12 hours) Store the cleared sample immersed in 100% (vol/vol) DBE in a dark, airtight glass vial to maintain transparency and preserve endogenous fluorescence.(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: Long-term)
6. Mount the sample in 100% (vol/vol) DBE for 3D light-sheet or confocal imaging.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hours)
