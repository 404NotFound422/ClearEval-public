Line 1 (Selected Fluorescence/Labeling Strategy + Clearing Method): **[NeuN + Alexa Fluor 647 (soma), β-III Tubulin (TUJ1) + Alexa Fluor 647 (axons/dendrites), TO-PRO-3 (whole-brain nuclear stain)] + iDISCO+ (DBE RI matching)**
Rationale for selection: iDISCO+ is suitable for rapid high-transparency clearing and light-sheet imaging of the complete adult mouse whole brain (~10×8×6 mm); high transparency and good contrast for fiber tract trajectories are achieved after organic solvent dehydration/delipidation; TO-PRO-3 and Alexa Fluor 647 signals are relatively robust in the iDISCO+ workflow, making them suitable for constructing 3D morphological maps of whole-brain neuronal somata and neurites.

**Protocol Steps:**

1 Pre-treatment and Dehydration
1.1 Soak in 20% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution, gentle agitation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
1.2 Soak in 40% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution, gentle agitation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
1.3 Soak in 60% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution, gentle agitation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
1.4 Soak in 80% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution, gentle agitation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
1.5 Soak in 100% (vol/vol) methanol, gentle agitation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
1.6 Soak again in fresh 100% (vol/vol) methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)

2 Bleaching/Dehemoglobinization (Bleaching, can significantly reduce whole-brain background)
2.1 Soak in 5% H2O2 in methanol (volume ratio: 30% H2O2 : methanol = 1 : 5), protected from light. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12–16 hours)

3 Rehydration
3.1 Soak in 80% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
3.2 Soak in 60% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
3.3 Soak in 40% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
3.4 Soak in 20% (vol/vol) methanol/water solution. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
3.5 Soak in 1×PBS. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)

4 Permeabilization & Blocking
4.1 Permeabilization solution: PBS + 0.2% (vol/vol) Triton X-100 + 20% (vol/vol) DMSO + 0.3 M Glycine, gentle agitation. (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)
4.2 Blocking solution: PBS + 0.2% Triton X-100 + 10% (vol/vol) DMSO + 6% (vol/vol) Normal Donkey Serum (NDS), gentle agitation. (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)

5 Whole-brain immunolabeling, primary
5.1 Primary antibody incubation solution: PBS + 0.2% Triton X-100 + 5% DMSO + 3% NDS + 0.01% (w/v) NaN3; add NeuN (RBFOX3) primary antibody at 1:200. (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 5–7 days)
5.2 Add β-III Tubulin (TUJ1) primary antibody at 1:300 to the same incubation solution (can be co-incubated in the same tube as 5.1). (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 5–7 days)

6 Wash after primary
6.1 Wash solution: PBS + 0.2% Triton X-100 + 0.01% NaN3, gentle agitation with solution changes. (Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
6.2 Repeat 6.1 for a total of 6 times (total wash approximately 36 hours). (Temperature: RT, Time: 36 hours)

7 Secondary, far-red
7.1 Secondary antibody incubation solution: PBS + 0.2% Triton X-100 + 3% NDS + 0.01% NaN3; add Donkey anti-mouse/rabbit (matched to primary antibody host) Alexa Fluor 647 secondary antibody at 1:400 (corresponding to NeuN and TUJ1 respectively; if the two primary antibodies have different hosts, they can be co-incubated in the same tube). (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 5–7 days, Light: protected)

8 Wash after secondary
8.1 Wash with PBS + 0.2% Triton X-100 + 0.01% NaN3, gentle agitation with solution changes. (Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
8.2 Repeat 8.1 for a total of 6 times (total wash approximately 36 hours). (Temperature: RT, Time: 36 hours)

9 Nuclear stain (TO-PRO-3, whole-brain nuclear localization for registration/segmentation assistance)
9.1 TO-PRO-3 1–2 µM (in PBS + 0.2% Triton X-100), protected from light, gentle agitation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 12–24 hours)
9.2 Wash with PBS 3 times. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours each)

10 Dehydration before clearing
10.1 20% methanol/water. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours) 40% methanol/water. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
10.3 60% methanol/water. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
10.4 80% methanol/water. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1.5 hours)
10.5 100% methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
10.6 Fresh 100% methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)

11 Delipidation with DCM
11.1 Soak in 66% (vol/vol) Dichloromethane (DCM)/33% methanol mixture. (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours)
11.2 Soak in 100% DCM. (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 × 1.5 hours)

12 Refractive index matching, DBE
12.1 Pre-soak in 100% Dibenzyl Ether (DBE, RI≈1.56). (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours, Light: protected)
12.2 Continue soaking in fresh 100% DBE until fully transparent (usually required for whole brain). (Temperature: RT, Time: 12–24 hours, Light: protected)

13 Storage & pre-imaging
13.1 Keep sample fully immersed in fresh 100% DBE, store sealed and protected from light (fill container as much as possible to reduce oxidation). (Temperature: RT or 4 ℃, Time: up to 1–2 weeks)
13.2 Replace with fresh DBE once before light-sheet imaging and allow to stand for degassing/sedimentation. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1–2 hours)
13.3 Match imaging medium and objective: use DBE-compatible imaging chamber and sealing materials with RI≈1.56; fix sample on a holder and proceed directly to whole-brain light-sheet scanning. (Temperature: RT, Time: per imaging session)
