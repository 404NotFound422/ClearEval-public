**Chosen Method:** ScaleS
**Chosen Labeling:** [GFP (内源) + Alexa Fluor 488, Phalloidin + Alexa Fluor 568]
**Justification:** ScaleS is an aqueous clearing method specifically optimized for high fluorescence preservation and quenching of autofluorescence, which is critical for the user's "weak endogenous fluorescence" samples. Unlike organic solvent methods (e.g., uDISCO) that often quench GFP, ScaleS maintains signal intensity and is compatible with long-term storage (up to 6 months or more) in ScaleS4(0) without significant degradation. The protocol allows for deep immunolabeling to boost the weak reporter signal (Anti-GFP) and simultaneous labeling of the dendritic cytoskeleton (Phalloidin) to visualize fine structures like spines and branches required for morphological analysis.

**Protocol Steps:**
1 Washing
1.1 Incubation with 1x PBS (Phosphate Buffered Saline) to remove residual fixative.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hours)
1.2 Incubation with fresh 1x PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hours)

2 Permeabilization and Delipidation
2.1 Incubation with ScaleS0 solution (4 M Urea, 10% w/v Glycerol, 0.1% vol/vol Triton X-100 in 0.1 M Phosphate Buffer, pH 7.5) with gentle shaking.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 96 hours)

3 Blocking
3.1 Incubation with Blocking Buffer (ScaleS0 solution containing 5% vol/vol Normal Goat Serum) with gentle shaking.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)

4 Primary Antibody Incubation
4.1 Incubation with Primary Antibody solution (Rabbit anti-GFP diluted 1:500 in Blocking Buffer) with gentle shaking to enhance weak endogenous signal.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 168 hours)

5 Washing
5.1 Incubation with ScaleS0 solution with gentle shaking to remove unbound primary antibodies.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)
5.2 Incubation with fresh ScaleS0 solution with gentle shaking.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 12 hours)

6 Secondary Antibody and Dye Staining
6.1 Incubation with Staining solution (Goat anti-Rabbit Alexa Fluor 488 diluted 1:500 and Phalloidin-Alexa Fluor 568 diluted 1:200 in Blocking Buffer) with gentle shaking, protected from light.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 120 hours)

7 Washing
7.1 Incubation with ScaleS0 solution with gentle shaking to remove unbound dyes/antibodies, protected from light.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)

8 Refractive Index Matching and Clearing
8.1 Incubation with ScaleS4 solution (4 M Urea, 30% w/v Glycerol, 0.1% vol/vol Triton X-100 in 0.1 M Phosphate Buffer, pH 7.5) with gentle shaking, protected from light.(Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)

9 Long-term Storage and Imaging Preparation
9.1 Incubation with ScaleS4(0) solution (ScaleS4 solution supplemented with 0.1% vol/vol Triethanolamine) without shaking, protected from light, for long-term storage and imaging.(Temperature: RT, Time: Until imaging or 6 months)
