**Chosen Method:** iDISCO+  
**Chosen Labeling:** [tdTomato (endogenous), DiI]  
**Justification:** iDISCO+ is well-suited for whole mouse brain clearing with strong fluorescence preservation, especially for robust fluorescent proteins like tdTomato. It uses a methanol-based dehydration and delipidation protocol that is fast and compatible with lipid-soluble dyes such as DiI, which can label axonal membranes effectively. This method allows for high transparency while maintaining the integrity of retrogradely labeled long-range projection neurons and their axonal architecture.

**Protocol Steps:**

1 Dehydration  
1.1 Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)  
1.2 Incubation with 50% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)  
1.3 Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)  
1.4 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol (Temperature: RT, Time: 12 hours)

2 Delipidation  
2.1 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol + 10% (vol/vol) DMSO + 2% (vol/vol) Triton X-100 (Temperature: RT, Time: 72 hours)  
2.2 Replace solution every 24 hours to ensure complete delipidation

3 Rehydration  
3.1 Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)  
3.2 Incubation with 50% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)  
3.3 Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)  
3.4 Incubation with PBS only (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)

4 Fluorescent labeling (DiI)  
4.1 Incubation with 1 μg/mL DiI in PBS (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)  
4.2 Wash 3 times with PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour each)

5 Optical Clearing (RI Matching)  
5.1 Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) RI-matching solution (iDISCO-R0: 20 vol% MXDA, 15% w/v sorbitol in PBS, RI = 1.40) (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
5.2 Incubation with 40% (vol/vol) RI-matching solution (iDISCO-R1: 40 vol% MXDA, 30% w/v sorbitol in PBS, RI = 1.48) (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
5.3 Incubation with 60% (vol/vol) RI-matching solution (iDISCO-R2: 60 vol% MXDA, 50% w/v sorbitol in PBS, RI = 1.51) (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
5.4 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) RI-matching solution (iDISCO-R3: 100 vol% MXDA, 50% w/v sorbitol in PBS, RI = 1.53) (Temperature: RT, Time: 48 hours)

6 Imaging Preparation  
6.1 Transfer sample into imaging chamber filled with iDISCO-R3 (Temperature: RT, Time: immediately ready for imaging)
