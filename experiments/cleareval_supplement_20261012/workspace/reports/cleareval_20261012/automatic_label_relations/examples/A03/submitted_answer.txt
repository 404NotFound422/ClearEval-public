**Chosen Method:** FDISCO
**Chosen Labeling:** [GFP (内源)+Alexa Fluor 488, MAP2+Alexa Fluor 594, DAPI]
**Justification:** FDISCO is specifically optimized for preserving weak endogenous fluorescence (like GFP/YFP) by maintaining an alkaline environment (pH 9.0) throughout the dehydration and clearing process, which prevents the quenching of fluorescent proteins common in other solvent-based methods. For a 3 mm thick brain block, FDISCO provides superior transparency and refractive index matching (RI ≈ 1.56) compared to aqueous methods, ensuring high-resolution 3D imaging of fine dendritic branches. The dual-labeling strategy uses Alexa Fluor 488 to boost the weak endogenous GFP signal and MAP2 to provide a detailed dendritic scaffold, while the pH-adjusted DBE allows for stable long-term storage (up to 6 months) at 4 ℃.

**Protocol Steps:**
1 Pre-clearing Immunostaining (Pre-treatment)
1.1 Incubation with PTx2 solution (PBS with 0.2% Triton X-100) for permeabilization. (Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
1.2 Incubation with Blocking Solution (PTx2, 10% Normal Goat Serum, and 5% DMSO). (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
1.3 Incubation with Primary Antibodies (Anti-GFP and Anti-MAP2, 1:500 dilution in PTwH: PBS with 0.2% Tween-20 and 10 μg/ml Heparin). (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 72 hours)
1.4 Incubation with PTwH solution for washing (5 changes). (Temperature: RT, Time: 10 hours)
1.5 Incubation with Secondary Antibodies (Alexa Fluor 488 and Alexa Fluor 594, 1:500) and DAPI (1:1000) in PTwH. (Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 72 hours)
1.6 Incubation with PTwH solution for final washing (5 changes). (Temperature: RT, Time: 10 hours)

2 Dehydration (FDISCO)
2.1 Incubation with 50% (vol/vol) Tetrahydrofuran (THF) in distilled water, pH adjusted to 9.0 with Triethylamine. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 2 hours)
2.2 Incubation with 70% (vol/vol) THF in distilled water, pH adjusted to 9.0 with Triethylamine. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 2 hours)
2.3 Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) THF in distilled water, pH adjusted to 9.0 with Triethylamine. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 2 hours)
2.4 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) THF (peroxide-free), pH adjusted to 9.0 with Triethylamine. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 2 hours)
2.5 Incubation with a second round of 100% (vol/vol) THF, pH adjusted to 9.0 with Triethylamine. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 2 hours)

3 Clearing and RI Matching
3.1 Incubation with Dibenzyl ether (DBE), pH adjusted to 9.0 with Triethylamine, until the tissue becomes completely transparent. (Temperature: RT, Time: 4 hours)

4 Preservation and Imaging
4.1 Storage in fresh pH-adjusted DBE in a glass vial, protected from light and sealed to prevent oxidation. (Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 6 months)
4.2 Pre-imaging mounting in a custom chamber filled with pH-adjusted DBE (RI = 1.56). (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
