**Chosen Method:** iDISCO+
**Chosen Labeling:** [tdTomato (endogenous), β-III Tubulin (TUJ1) + Alexa Fluor 647]
**Justification:** iDISCO+ is ideal for whole adult mouse brains due to its rapid delipidation and strong compatibility with red-shifted fluorescent proteins like tdTomato, which is critical for preserving the AAV-driven retrograde labeling signal. It also allows effective immunolabeling of cytoskeletal targets such as β-III Tubulin (a pan-neuronal axon marker) using Alexa Fluor 647-conjugated secondary antibodies, which are highly stable under iDISCO+ organic solvent conditions. The method induces moderate tissue shrinkage (~20–30%), which is acceptable per the problem statement, and achieves high transparency within days—enabling fast light-sheet imaging.
**Protocol Steps:**
Dehydration
Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 40% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 60% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
2 Bleaching (optional but recommended for endogenous fluorescence preservation)
Incubation with chilled fresh 5% (vol/vol) H₂O₂ in methanol (1 volume 30% H₂O₂ to 5 volumes MeOH).(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)
3 Rehydration
Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 60% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 40% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with PBS + 0.2% (vol/vol) Triton X-100 (PBT).(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Permeabilization and Blocking
Incubation with PBT + 20% (vol/vol) DMSO + 0.3 M glycine.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)
Incubation with PBT + 10% (vol/vol) DMSO + 6% (wt/vol) donkey serum (blocking buffer).(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)
5 Primary Antibody Staining
Incubation with anti-β-III Tubulin (TUJ1) primary antibody diluted 1:500 in blocking buffer.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 72 hours)
6 Wash
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
7 Secondary Antibody Staining
Incubation with Alexa Fluor 647-conjugated donkey anti-mouse IgG (H+L) diluted 1:300 in blocking buffer.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 48 hours)
8 Wash
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
Incubation with PBT.(Temperature: RT, Time: 6 hours)
9 Dehydration (post-immunostaining)
Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 40% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 60% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in PBS.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol.(Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)
Delipidation
Incubation with dichloromethane (DCM).(Temperature: RT, Time: 30 minutes)
Incubation with dichloromethane (DCM).(Temperature: RT, Time: 30 minutes)
Refractive Index Matching
Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) dibenzyl ether (DBE, Sigma 108014-1KG) without shaking.(Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)
12 Storage and Imaging Preparation
Transfer brain to glass vial filled with fresh DBE and store at RT in the dark until light-sheet imaging.(Temperature: RT, Time: ≤7 days)
