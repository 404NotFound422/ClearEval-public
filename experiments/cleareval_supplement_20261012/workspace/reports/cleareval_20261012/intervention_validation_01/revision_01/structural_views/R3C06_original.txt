**Chosen Method:** CUBIC  
**Chosen Labeling:** [GFP (内源), MAP2 + Alexa Fluor 488, DAPI]  
**Justification:** CUBIC is an aqueous-based, non-denaturing clearing method that excels at preserving weak endogenous fluorescent proteins (e.g., GFP) over long-term storage, unlike organic solvent–based methods (e.g., iDISCO+, uDISCO) which often quench or irreversibly damage such signals. It provides excellent transparency for 3 mm tissue blocks within days and enables stable refractive index (RI ≈ 1.49) matching compatible with light-sheet and confocal microscopy. The dual-labeling strategy leverages the sparse endogenous GFP reporter to identify individual neurons, while immunolabeling against MAP2 (a dendritic cytoskeletal marker) with Alexa Fluor 488 reinforces dendritic arborization and structural integrity; DAPI provides nuclear counterstain for spatial registration and cell density context. All fluorophores used are known to be well-preserved in CUBIC, and the cleared samples can be stored in CUBIC-R+ for ≥6 months without significant signal decay.

**Protocol Steps:**  
1 Post-fixation Stabilization  
1.1 Incubation with 4% (wt/vol) paraformaldehyde (PFA) in PBS (pH 7.4) to confirm fixation completeness.(Temperature: 4 ℃, Time: 12 hours)  

2 Permeabilization and Blocking  
2.1 Incubation with CUBIC-P1 (10 vol% Triton X-100, 10 vol% Tween-20, 10 wt% sucrose in PBS).(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)  
2.2 Incubation with CUBIC-B (10 vol% DMSO, 5 vol% normal goat serum, 0.5 wt% sodium azide in PBS).(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 12 hours)  

3 Primary Antibody Labeling  
3.1 Incubation with mouse anti-MAP2 primary antibody (1:500 dilution in CUBIC-B).(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 72 hours)  

4 Washing  
4.1 Incubation with CUBIC-W (0.5 vol% Triton X-100 in PBS) with gentle shaking.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)  
4.2 Repeat step 4.1 two additional times, changing solution every 8 hours.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours total)  

5 Secondary Antibody Labeling  
5.1 Incubation with goat anti-mouse IgG conjugated to Alexa Fluor 488 (1:300 dilution in CUBIC-B).(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 48 hours)  

6 Nuclear Counterstaining  
6.1 Incubation with DAPI (1 µg/mL in CUBIC-W).(Temperature: RT, Time: 12 hours)  

7 Washing Prior to Clearing  
7.1 Incubation with CUBIC-W.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 24 hours)  

8 Tissue Clearing (Delipidation & Decolorization)  
8.1 Incubation with CUBIC-L (25 wt% Quadrol, 25 wt% urea, 15 wt% sucrose, 10 vol% Triton X-100 in H₂O, pH 9.0–9.5).(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 72 hours)  
8.2 Replace with fresh CUBIC-L.(Temperature: 37 ℃, Time: 48 hours)  

9 Refractive Index Matching  
9.1 Incubation with CUBIC-R+ (50 wt% sucrose, 25 wt% urea, 10 wt% triethanolamine, 0.5 vol% Triton X-100 in H₂O, RI = 1.49).(Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
9.2 Replace with fresh CUBIC-R+ for final storage and imaging.(Temperature: RT, Time: ≥6 months)  

10 Imaging Preparation  
10.1 Transfer sample to glass-bottom imaging chamber filled with fresh CUBIC-R+ immediately before imaging.(Temperature: RT, Time: <1 hour prior to acquisition)
