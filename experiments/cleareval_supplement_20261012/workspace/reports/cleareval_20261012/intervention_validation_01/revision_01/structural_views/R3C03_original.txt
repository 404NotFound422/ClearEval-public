**Chosen Method:** uDISCO
**Chosen Labeling:** [NeuN (RBFOX3)+Alexa Fluor 647, beta-III Tubulin (TUJ1)+Alexa Fluor 488, DAPI]
**Justification:** uDISCO is selected for its superior capability to clear whole adult mouse brains rapidly while achieving high transparency (RI ~1.56) and significant tissue shrinkage (~65%). The shrinkage is advantageous for imaging large volumes like a whole brain faster and with higher resolution. The method preserves fluorescence signals well and is compatible with immunolabeling protocols. The chosen markers—NeuN for neuronal somata and beta-III Tubulin for axons and dendrites—combined with DAPI, provide a comprehensive map of neuronal morphology and distribution required by the problem statement.
