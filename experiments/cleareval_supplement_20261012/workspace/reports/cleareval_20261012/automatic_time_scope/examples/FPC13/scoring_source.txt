**Chosen Method:** CUBIC  
**Chosen Labeling:** [GFP, MAP2+Alexa Fluor 488]  
**Justification:** CUBIC is a water-based clearing method that preserves endogenous fluorescence well, making it ideal for weak signals such as sparse GFP-labeled neurons. It is gentle on tissue and compatible with long-term storage in CUBIC-R solution. The combination of endogenous GFP and exogenous MAP2 staining (to highlight dendritic structures) provides dual labeling to visualize both neuronal somata and dendritic morphology in 3D. This strategy supports detailed morphological analysis while minimizing photobleaching and signal loss.

**Protocol Steps:**

1 Dehydration  
1.1 Incubation with 10% (vol/vol) glycerol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 12 hours)  
1.2 Incubation with 20% (vol/vol) glycerol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 12 hours)  
1.3 Incubation with 30% (vol/vol) glycerol in PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 12 hours)

2 Wash  
2.1 Incubation with PBS (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)

3 Delipidation  
3.1 Incubation with CUBIC-U (50 mM NaCl, 100 mM choline chloride, 0.5% wt/vol TX-100, 0.2% wt/vol BSA in PBS) (Temperature: 37 °C, Time: 48 hours)  
3.2 Replace CUBIC-U every 24 hours; repeat incubation for total 72 hours

4 Refractive Index Matching  
4.1 Incubation with CUBIC-R (50 mM NaCl, 100 mM choline chloride, 0.2% wt/vol BSA in PBS) (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)

5 Post-Clearing Staining (Optional Enhancement)  
5.1 Incubation with 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS for permeabilization (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
5.2 Incubation with 1% BSA in PBS for blocking (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
5.3 Incubation with primary antibody: anti-MAP2 (1:500 dilution in 1% BSA/PBS) (Temperature: 4 °C, Time: 48 hours)  
5.4 Incubation with secondary antibody: Alexa Fluor 488-conjugated anti-IgG (1:1000 dilution in 1% BSA/PBS) (Temperature: RT, Time: 2 hours)  
5.5 Incubation with PBS for washing (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 × 1 hour)

6 Final Refractive Index Matching  
6.1 Incubation with CUBIC-R (50 mM NaCl, 100 mM choline chloride, 0.2% wt/vol BSA in PBS) (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)

7 Long-Term Storage / Imaging Preparation  
7.1 Store in CUBIC-R at 4 °C for long-term preservation or proceed to imaging  
7.2 For light-sheet microscopy, ensure RI matching by confirming no bubbles or phase mismatch in the sample before imaging
