**Chosen Method:** uDISCO  
**Chosen Labeling:** [tdTomato, Phalloidin+Alexa Fluor 647]  
**Justification:** uDISCO is selected for its rapid clearing speed (3-5 days for whole mouse brain) and high transparency, which meets the requirement to complete imaging as soon as possible. It is compatible with tdTomato fluorescence preservation and allows for some tissue shrinkage, which is acceptable per the problem statement. Phalloidin conjugated to Alexa Fluor 647 is chosen to label the axonal cytoskeleton (F-actin), providing the necessary structural contrast while being spectrally distinct from tdTomato.

**Protocol Steps:**  
1 Dehydration  
1.1 Incubation with 50% (vol/vol) methanol in distilled water. (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours)  
1.2 Incubation with 80% (vol/vol) methanol in distilled water. (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours)  
1.3 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours)  
1.4 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours)  
1.5 Incubation with 100% (vol/vol) dichloromethane (DCM). (Temperature: RT, Time: 30 minutes)  

2 Labeling (Axonal Skeleton)  
2.1 Incubation with staining solution containing Phalloidin conjugated to Alexa Fluor 647 (1:200 dilution) in methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 24 hours)  
2.2 Wash with 100% (vol/vol) methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  
2.3 Wash with 100% (vol/vol) methanol. (Temperature: RT, Time: 1 hour)  

3 Refractive Index Matching & Clearing  
3.1 Incubation with BABB-D (1 part Benzyl Alcohol, 2 parts Benzyl Benzoate, 0.04% w/v Diphenyl ether). (Temperature: RT, Time: 3 hours)  
3.2 Incubation with fresh BABB-D. (Temperature: RT, Time: Until tissue is transparent, typically 12-24 hours)  

4 Storage & Imaging Preparation  
4.1 Store sample in fresh BABB-D at 4°C protected from light until imaging. (Temperature: 4°C, Time: Long-term)
